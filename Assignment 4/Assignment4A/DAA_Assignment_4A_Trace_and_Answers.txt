TASK 4 — WORD BREAK FORENSICS
Faulty condition checks only whether Text[j..i-1] is a dictionary word.
Counterexample: Text="ab", Dictionary={"b"}.
DP[0]=TRUE; DP[1]=FALSE; at i=2, substring "b" is found and faulty logic
sets DP[2]=TRUE even though prefix "a" cannot be segmented.
Minimal repair: require BOTH DP[j]=TRUE AND substring(Text,j,i) in Dictionary.
Regression: Text="apple", Dictionary={"app","le"} remains TRUE:
DP[3] is made true by "app"; DP[5] is made true by "le" after DP[3].

Dijkstra:

Process B: C=6, D=10.
